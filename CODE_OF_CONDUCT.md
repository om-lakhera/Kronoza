# Code of Conduct

Kronoza aims to be a respectful and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, disability, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and open to good-faith discussion.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces used for Kronoza community work. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
